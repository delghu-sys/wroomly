# Pricing — Wroomly

Wroomly is a sublet and student-housing marketplace for the University of
Michigan community in Ann Arbor (https://wroomly.app).

## Current pricing

- Browsing listings: free
- Posting a listing: free
- Sending inquiries and messaging: free
- Wroomly Match (AI concierge alerts): free
- UMich verification (blue check via @umich.edu Google sign-in): free

There are no fees of any kind today. Wroomly does not process payments:
rent and deposits are arranged directly between students, outside the
platform.

Pricing current as of 2026-08-31. See https://wroomly.app/terms for the
terms that govern the service.
