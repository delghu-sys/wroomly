## 6. Fair housing — this one is not optional

**Federal, Michigan, and Ann Arbor law prohibit housing advertisements that state or indicate a preference, limitation, or discrimination based on a protected characteristic.**

Under the federal Fair Housing Act these include race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, and disability. Michigan's Elliott-Larsen Civil Rights Act adds age, marital status, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, height, and weight. Ann Arbor's ordinance further protects source of income, including housing vouchers.

**You may not post any listing, profile, or message on Wroomly — or ask Wroomly Match to filter for — any preference, limitation, or exclusion based on these characteristics.** This applies even where you believe an exemption might cover your particular living situation. Prohibited examples include "no kids," "Christians preferred," "students only," "no Section 8," "young professionals," "must be female," and "English speakers only."

Describe the property and the terms — bedrooms, rent, dates, amenities, parking, quiet hours, smoking, pets — not the kind of person you want.

We remove violating content and may permanently ban accounts that post it. We may report violations to the Fair Housing Center of Southeast & Mid Michigan, HUD, or the Michigan Department of Civil Rights.
